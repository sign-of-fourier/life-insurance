1. [CONTRADICTION] TOB-2.1 vs. TOB-3.1 — TOB-2.1 states any cigarette use within 12 months places an applicant on the tobacco ladder with a best class of Standard Tobacco. TOB-3.1 allows ≤ 12 cigars per year with a negative cotinine to be rated on the non-tobacco ladder with a best class of Preferred. Suggested fix: Align the best class for cigars with the same rules as cigarettes if used within 12 months.
